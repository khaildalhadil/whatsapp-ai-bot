Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This WhatsApp bot is a student final-year project. This page explains what happens to your data when you message it.

What we receive

When you send a message to the bot, the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) sends us your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the content of your message.

How we use it

We use this data only to read your message and send you a reply. The replies are written by an AI: the text of your message is sent to OpenAI to generate the answer.

Storage and sharing

To understand follow-up questions, the bot keeps your last few messages in the server's memory. They are erased whenever the server restarts. We do not store your messages in a database, and we do not sell your data. Your messages are only shared with the services needed to answer you: Meta's WhatsApp service, and OpenAI for the AI reply. Each of them has its own privacy policy. Messages may appear briefly in server logs for debugging.

Deleting your data

You can stop messaging the bot at any time. To ask us to delete any data related to you, send the bot a message asking for it.

Contact

You can contact us through the same WhatsApp number as the bot.
